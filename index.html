How it works

The math, kept out of sight.

You set the goals

Budget, calories, protein, carbs, fat, fiber, optional minerals and vitamins, plus what you eat and what you'd rather skip.

An optimizer does the sums

A mixed-integer linear program picks how many half-servings of each food to buy. It must stay inside your ranges and budget, cap any one food's share of calories, and keep portions practical. Your priorities adjust its settings. Nothing is guessed by AI.

If it can't fit, it tells you why

It builds the closest plan within budget, finds the budget that would work, and offers one-tap changes.

Maths, not medical advice

Quantities, costs and nutrient totals are calculated from the food database. Whether a target suits your body is outside what this tool can judge. For health conditions, pregnancy, children or medication, ask a doctor or registered dietitian.
